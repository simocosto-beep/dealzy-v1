Explore by city and trip style

Miami is a strong starting point for beaches, nightlife, dining and water activities. New York works well for museums, shows, neighbourhood food and iconic attractions, while Los Angeles combines entertainment, coastal experiences and day trips. Chicago is useful for architecture, food and lakefront activities; Las Vegas for entertainment and excursions; and Orlando for theme parks and family travel.

Use Dealzy to compare what is available for the city and date you have in mind. For restaurants, check opening hours and reservation rules. For activities, confirm meeting points, age limits and cancellation terms. For hotels and flights, always review the final provider price, taxes and conditions before paying.

How Dealzy works
