UNITED STATES · SUN & COAST

Miami: slow mornings, lively evenings

A flexible weekend combining sunshine, neighborhoods and time near the water. Weather can change quickly, so keep outdoor activities easy to rearrange.

Day one · Find your rhythm

Start with a relaxed morning, explore a walkable neighborhood, and choose one cultural or food experience. Leave the evening open for a scenic waterfront stroll or dinner.

Day two · Something on the water

Make room for a beach, a boat activity, or a calmer local outing. Confirm departure points and weather policies before committing to an excursion.

UNITED STATES · CULTURE & CITY LIFE

New York: one neighborhood at a time

There is no need to cross the whole city in forty-eight hours. Choose a couple of areas you genuinely want to explore and allow for public transport time.

Day one · Streets and stories

Pick a neighborhood for coffee, galleries, bookstores or architecture, then consider one ticketed activity for later. Reserve popular attractions only after comparing their terms.

Day two · A different perspective

Take a walk by a park or waterfront, enjoy a long lunch, and add a museum or observation experience if it suits your interests and budget.

CANADA · FOOD & DISCOVERY

Toronto: big-city energy, easy detours

Combine city sights with one relaxed food or waterfront stop. Check public transport and local events before choosing where to stay.

Day one · Discover the city

Explore a neighborhood with cafés and shops, spend time around a cultural landmark, and pick dinner based on your preferred cuisine instead of chasing too many stops.

Day two · Outdoors or indoors

Consider a waterfront walk, a museum or a nearby day activity depending on the weather. Compare ticket details and travel time before booking.

CANADA · FOOD & CULTURE

Montréal: take the scenic route

Montréal is a city to explore on foot when the weather allows. Mix heritage streets, neighborhood cafés, art and a little room for spontaneity.

Day one · A leisurely wander

Begin in a walkable historic area and follow your curiosity toward small shops or public spaces. Choose your evening meal in advance if it's a busy weekend.

Day two · A local moment

Explore a market or another neighborhood, then consider an indoor experience when the season calls for it. Check bilingual information and opening hours with providers.
