About Dealzy AI

Dealzy helps people explore experiences, events and travel options in the United States and Canada. You can search by city and budget, save ideas and organize a trip.

What you see in a search

Available provider results and external partner links serve different purposes. A result can include a price or event information returned by a provider. A partner link takes you elsewhere to search or book; it does not establish that a particular price or room is available. Coverage varies by city and category.

Make a comparison, not just a click

Check the currency, number of people, dates and included services before comparing prices. A low starting price can exclude fees or apply only to selected dates. Our offer comparison guide includes a worked example and a booking checklist.

Limits of the service

Dealzy is not a booking agent or the seller of partner products. It does not cover every provider and cannot guarantee the cheapest offer. The merchant confirms the final price, availability and cancellation policy. AI suggestions can contain mistakes and should be checked against the offer details.

How Dealzy is funded

Eligible partner purchases may generate a commission. Read our affiliate disclosure. A listed merchant or a paid relationship is not an independent quality certification.
