How to compare offers before booking

A headline discount is only one part of a booking decision. Compare what you will actually pay and receive for the same dates and group size.

1. Make the offers comparable

Choose the same date, time, number of adults and children, and service level. For an activity, check its duration, meeting point, transport, equipment and guide language. For accommodation, compare the same number of nights, room occupancy and meal plan. If one option includes transport and another does not, add your transport estimate separately.

2. Calculate the total, in one currency

Use the final checkout amount when available. Otherwise list the base cost, known mandatory charges and any costs still unknown. Keep USD and CAD separate until you have a current conversion rate and know any card conversion fees. An unknown fee is not zero.

Illustrative example — not a live offer.

For two adults, option A costs $45 per person plus a $12 fee per booking: 2 × $45 + $12 = $102. Option B costs $49 per person with the same services and no additional fee: 2 × $49 = $98. Option B is $4 cheaper overall despite its higher headline price. Any taxes not included in those figures still need to be added.

3. Check whether the discount is meaningful

For a verified reference price of $120 and a comparable final price of $96, the saving is $24, or 20%: (120 − 96) ÷ 120 × 100. This calculation is useful only if both prices cover the same thing. A crossed-out price alone does not establish what the product normally sells for. If no reliable reference price is available, compare totals without claiming a percentage saving.

4. Value flexibility

Read the cancellation deadline, its time zone and any non-refundable portion. A cheaper, non-refundable booking may be a poor fit if your dates could change. Check what happens if the provider cancels or weather affects the activity. Save the terms displayed at purchase with your confirmation.

5. Verify before paying

  • Confirm the exact date, location and participants.
  • Check the currency and full amount payable.
  • Read exclusions, cancellation and refund conditions.
  • Confirm that the final merchant page matches the offer you selected.
  • Use the merchant confirmation as the record of your booking.
