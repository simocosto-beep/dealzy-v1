Privacy

Dealzy is an early product build. This page explains the current behavior of the app.

Data stored on your device

Favorites, trip items, toolbox preferences, local alert rules and an optional cached location coordinate are stored in your browser using local storage. Clearing site data removes these local records.

Optional accounts and cloud storage

If you create an account, authentication is handled through Supabase. Your account may include your email address, display name and phone number if provided. Signing in stores session credentials in your browser. Cloud sync can store saved offers, trips, alerts, price watches, coupons and preferences associated with your account. Clearing browser data does not delete cloud records.

For questions about account data or a deletion request, use our contact page. Do not include your password or authentication codes.

Location

Dealzy asks for browser location permission only when you choose to use location features. You can deny or revoke that permission in your browser settings.

Hosting and API requests

The site is hosted on Vercel. Standard request metadata may be processed by the hosting platform when pages or API endpoints are requested.

Advertising

Dealzy may show advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google and other advertising partners may place or read cookies and use device identifiers, IP addresses or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. Ad selection may use your visits to this and other websites. You can manage Google ad personalization in My Ad Center and learn more about Google's use of information on Google's partner sites page. If a consent notice is displayed, you can use it to choose how advertising data is processed.

Partners

Dealzy searches connected providers for live results. If no live offers are available in a market, the app may show an empty state. Any demo fallback is labeled and is not presented as a live partner offer.

Important: Do not enter passwords, payment card information or other sensitive personal data into the assistant.

Optional AI assistant

If you open the assistant and send a message, your message, a short history of that conversation, your selected city and a small set of public offer details are sent to Google Gemini to generate a response. Dealzy does not send precise GPS coordinates or account details in that request. The conversation is kept in this page until you reload it. Google may use Free tier content to improve its products; do not enter sensitive information.
